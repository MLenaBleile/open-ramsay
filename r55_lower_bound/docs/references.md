# References

## Primary Sources

### Ramsey Number Bounds

1. **Radziszowski, S. P.** (2024). Small Ramsey Numbers. *Electronic Journal of Combinatorics*, Dynamic Survey DS1.
   - URL: https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/DS1
   - The definitive reference for current bounds on small Ramsey numbers
   - Continuously updated

2. **McKay, B. D., & Radziszowski, S. P.** (1995). R(4,5) = 25. *Journal of Graph Theory*, 19(3), 309-322.

3. **Angeltveit, V., & McKay, B. D.** (2017). R(5,5) ≤ 48. *Journal of Graph Theory*, 89(1), 5-13.
   - Improved upper bound through computational methods

### Paley Graph Construction

4. **Paley, R. E. A. C.** (1933). On orthogonal matrices. *Journal of Mathematics and Physics*, 12, 311-320.

5. **Bollobás, B.** (1998). *Modern Graph Theory*. Springer.
   - Chapter on Ramsey theory and Paley graph properties

### Computational Methods

6. **Exoo, G.** Ramsey Number Constructions.
   - URL: http://ginger.indstate.edu/ge/RAMSEY/
   - Computational lower bounds and explicit constructions

7. **Codish, M., Frank, M., Itzhakov, A., & Miller, A.** (2016). Computing the Ramsey Number R(4,3,3) using Abstraction and Symmetry Breaking. *Constraints*, 21(3), 375-393.

### SAT-based Approaches

8. **Heule, M. J. H.** (2018). Schur Number Five. *Proceedings of AAAI*.
   - SAT techniques for combinatorial problems

### Textbooks

9. **Graham, R. L., Rothschild, B. L., & Spencer, J. H.** (1990). *Ramsey Theory* (2nd ed.). Wiley-Interscience.

10. **Diestel, R.** (2017). *Graph Theory* (5th ed.). Springer.
    - GTM 173, Chapter 9 on Ramsey Theory
